### 2.1 What the flag column does and does not mean

The check is a token comparison run by `docs/architecture/measure-c06.ps1` conventions: it extracts numbers, units
and threshold phrases from the wording, then looks for them in the concatenated source text of the cited rules.
A token that is absent from the source is flagged. It then compares the length of the wording against the source.

**This is triage, not adjudication.** A flag means "read this row against its source". It does **not** mean the row
is wrong, and `N` does **not** mean the row is right - it means no token was found that is absent from the source.
Only you can decide. `GAP-046` in section 5 is the part where a human read has already found a real problem.

### 5.1 `RT-475` in particular

`RT-475` currently asserts that "a negative stock balance is a store policy decision and is separate from the input
rule". That clause is not in `BI-05` ("Entry quantities are never negative") or `IV-34` ("Adjustments are
one-directional per line: a line is an increase or a decrease, with a non-negative quantity"). The clause belongs to
`SM-14`, which is homed at `RT-410`. The proposed correction removes the clause from `RT-475` rather than
duplicating `RT-410` here. Removing it loses nothing: `RT-410` already carries that behaviour.
